About the unit

Discipline that outlasts the uniform

The NCC is the youth wing of India's armed forces, open to students in colleges and schools. At MIT-WPU, cadets train alongside their degree: parade and weapon training, map reading, first aid, leadership, and social service.

Cadets can appear for the NCC "A", "B" and "C" certificate examinations, take part in national camps, and gain an edge when applying to the Services.

  1. Register
    Fill in the online form below.
  2. Verify
    Documents and fitness are checked by the ANO.
  3. Train
    Weekly parades, drills and classes.
  4. Camp & certify
    Attend camps and sit certificate exams.

Questions

FAQ

Who can join?

Enrolled MIT-WPU students who meet the NCC eligibility and medical requirements. Final selection is by the unit's officers.

Will it clash with my classes?

Training is scheduled around the academic timetable; the unit will share the parade schedule after enrolment.

What are the A, B and C certificates?

Progressive NCC certificate examinations taken after completing the required years of training and camps.

Is there a cost?

Contact the unit for current details on uniform and camp requirements.
